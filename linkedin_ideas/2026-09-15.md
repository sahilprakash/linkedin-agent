## Idea 2 — Microsoft Fabric & Analytics Engineering
**Topic:** Dataflows vs. Pipelines: Choosing Wisely

**Hook:** Confused between dataflows and pipelines? Let’s clear the fog.

**Full Draft:**

Confused between dataflows and pipelines? Let’s clear the fog.

Dataflows are great for quick transformations directly in Power BI Service, but they’re limited to 2 GB per flow and don’t scale well for heavy ETL. Pipelines, on the other hand, let you orchestrate multiple dataflows, run them on schedule, and even trigger downstream actions.

In a recent project, I stitched together a pipeline that pulled raw logs from Azure Blob, ran a dataflow to clean and deduplicate, then pushed the gold dataset into OneLake for analytics. The pipeline’s retry logic cut failure rates by 40%.

Rule of thumb: use dataflows for small, interactive datasets and pipelines when you need repeatable, production‑grade workflows that hit the lakehouse.

Which one are you using right now for your next project?
