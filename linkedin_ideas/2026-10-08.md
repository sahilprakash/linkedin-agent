## Idea 5 — Power BI & DAX
**Topic:** CALCULATE Tricks That Change Your Measures

**Hook:** One CALCULATE tweak can flip your KPI.

**Full Draft:**

One CALCULATE tweak can flip your KPI.

When you need a month‑over‑month growth that ignores slicer filters, wrap the measure in:

```
Growth =
    CALCULATE(
        DIVIDE([Current Sales] - [Prior Sales], [Prior Sales]),
        ALLSELECTED('Date'),
        'Date'[Month] = MAX('Date'[Month]) - 1
    )
```

The key is the ALLSELECTED to keep user‑chosen filters (like region) while forcing the date context to the previous month. I used this pattern to replace a messy series of IF statements in a sales dashboard, cutting DAX lines by 60 % and improving readability.

Try swapping a complex IF chain with a single CALCULATE‑based measure and watch the formula simplify.
