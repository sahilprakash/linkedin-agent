## Idea 3 — Microsoft Fabric & Analytics Engineering
**Topic:** Medallion Architecture in Fabric

**Hook:** Medallion architecture keeps data clean and trustworthy.

**Full Draft:**

Medallion architecture keeps data clean and trustworthy.

In Fabric, I built a three‑tier lakehouse: raw, bronze, and silver. The raw tier holds unfiltered feeds. Bronze adds minimal cleansing—deduplication, basic type casting. Silver applies business rules, joins, and aggregates.

The key is to keep each layer immutable. When a new source arrives, I create a new raw table, then run a deterministic transformation into bronze. Silver tables are versioned with a date suffix, so rollbacks are trivial.

I exposed the silver layer through a semantic model that Power BI uses. Because the model sits on a stable, auditable source, users trust the numbers.

How do you structure your data layers? Share your medallion strategy.
