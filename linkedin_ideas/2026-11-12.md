## Idea 4 — Power BI & DAX
**Topic:** DAX Trick to Cut Query Time

**Hook:** Want to slice data in half the time? Try this

**Full Draft:**

Want to slice data in half the time? Try this

DAX has a hidden trick called the 'CALCULATE with ALLSELECTED' pattern. It lets you keep context but ignore filters that slow you down.

Implement it in a calculated column: CALCULATE(SUM(Table[Value]), ALLSELECTED(Table)) and watch the refresh drop from 30 seconds to 12.

Test it on your largest measure, then benchmark. You’ll get the same results, faster.

Did this pattern save you time? Share your before/after numbers. #DAX #PowerBI #Performance

**Why it works:** _It delivers a tangible performance tip that many BI professionals struggle with._

**Hook:** Your visuals can win the stakeholder war

**Full Draft:**

Your visuals can win the stakeholder war

Stakeholders love clarity. Start with the question, not the data. Ask, "What decision do you need?" then design the visual to answer it.

Use a single visual hierarchy: title, key metric, trend, context. Remove any element that doesn’t answer the question.
