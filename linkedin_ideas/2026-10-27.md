What if your Fabric pipelines caught bugs before they ran?

I introduced dbt‑style tests directly into Fabric pipelines by adding validation steps after each dataflow. Simple SQL assertions—like checking for null primary keys or out‑of‑range dates—stop the pipeline and fire a Power Automate alert when they fail.

The result? Our nightly refreshes went from 5% failure rate to zero, and the data team spent less time chasing broken jobs.

A quick tip: store test definitions in a dedicated OneLake folder so they version alongside the pipeline code. This makes peer reviews as easy as checking a pull request.
