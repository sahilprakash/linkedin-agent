## Idea 2 — Microsoft Fabric & Analytics Engineering
**Topic:** Dataflows vs Pipelines: When to Use Each

**Hook:** Your Fabric pipeline choice can shave hours off ETL.

**Full Draft:**

Your Fabric pipeline choice can shave hours off ETL.

I once built a nightly pipeline for a sales feed, only to discover the same logic could live in a Dataflow and run on demand. Dataflows excel at reusable transformations; Pipelines shine when you need orchestrated jobs across services.

Rule of thumb: If the step is pure data shaping, push it to a Dataflow. If you need to trigger external APIs, schedule retries, or combine multiple services, wrap it in a Pipeline.
