## Idea 2 — Microsoft Fabric & Analytics Engineering
**Topic:** Dataflows vs Pipelines: When to Choose Each

**Hook:** Stop guessing—pick the right Fabric tool today.

**Full Draft:**

Stop guessing—pick the right Fabric tool today.

Dataflows shine when you need reusable transformations that power multiple reports. They live in OneLake, are versioned, and surface as tables for any downstream model.

Pipelines, on the other hand, excel at orchestrating complex jobs: moving data across regions, triggering external APIs, or running custom Python scripts. Think of pipelines as the conductor, dataflows as the musicians.

My recent project used dataflows for daily sales cleansing and a pipeline to copy the cleaned set into a Snowflake warehouse for heavy‑weight modeling. The split saved 30% runtime and kept the architecture easy to audit.

Do you prefer a dataflow‑first or pipeline‑first approach in your Fabric projects?
