Turn raw data into gold in three layers.

Why dataflows are your secret analytics engine.

During a recent Fabric rollout, I let data engineers focus on dataflows for ETL and handed the cleaned tables to BI developers to create semantic models. The split kept each team in its comfort zone and cut delivery time by 40%.

Key advantages:
• Dataflows handle source connectivity, refresh schedules, and lineage automatically.
• Semantic models add business logic, hierarchies, and role‑level security without touching the raw tables.
• OneLake stores everything, so the hand‑off is just a shared workspace.

The trick is to define clear contracts: expose only the columns needed for analysis and version the dataflow output. Then, let the Power BI team build reusable measures on top of the semantic model.
