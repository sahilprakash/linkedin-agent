**Full Draft:**

Medallion architecture is a game changer—here’s why.

In Fabric, I defined bronze for raw ingestion, silver for cleaned and enriched data, and gold for business‑ready aggregates. Each layer lives in its own dataflow, and I enforce lineage by tagging tables with layer metadata. The pipeline automatically promotes data when it passes quality checks.

This structure keeps the data lake tidy, speeds up query performance, and gives analysts a clear path to trustworthy insights. I even added a simple audit table that logs every promotion—no more guesswork.
