**Topic:** Speed Up Large Tables with Smart Measures

**Hook:** My report went from minutes to seconds.

**Full Draft:**

A client’s sales report loaded in 4 minutes because every visual used a calculated column that scanned 2 million rows. I swapped those columns for measures that aggregate on the fly, and added SUMX with a FILTER that runs only on the visible slice.

Key tip: keep row‑level calculations out of the model; let DAX do the work at query time.

## Idea 5 — Power BI & DAX
**Topic:** Calculated Column vs Measure: When to Choose

**Hook:** Choosing the wrong one adds hidden load.

**Full Draft:**

A colleague added a calculated column to flag “high‑value customers.” The column scanned the entire fact table, bloating the model. I suggested a measure that evaluates the flag only for the current filter context.

Result: model size shrank by 25 GB and the report refreshed in half the time.

Rule of thumb: use calculated columns for static attributes that don’t change with filters; use measures for anything that depends on slicers or visual interactions.
