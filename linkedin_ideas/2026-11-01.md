Send daily BI reports without lifting a finger

I built a flow that pulls the latest Power BI dataset, runs a DAX query, and emails a snapshot to the sales team every morning. The trigger is a simple schedule, the action is a Power Automate 'Run a dataset refresh', and the final step uses 'Send an email with attachment' with dynamic content.

## Idea 5 — Secondary: Career & Job Hunt – BI interview experiences
**Topic:** Cracking the BI Interview: My Top Tips

**Hook:** The first question was always 'Show me a dashboard'

**Full Draft:**

The first question was always "Show me a dashboard"

I learned early that the interview starts with a portfolio walk‑through. Pick a project that highlights data modeling, DAX, and storytelling. Explain the business goal, the data sources, and how the visuals answer key questions.

Next, expect technical puzzles: rewrite a DAX measure, optimize a query, or debug a data refresh issue. Practice with sample problems and be ready to explain your thought process.

Soft skills matter too: describe how you collaborated with analysts, how you handled stakeholder feedback, and how you used Power Platform to extend a solution.

What’s your toughest interview question? Share your experience and let’s help each other.
