## Idea 3 — Copilot Studio & Power Platform
**Topic:** Choose measures over calculated columns

**Hook:** One DAX decision can make your model easier to maintain.

**Full Draft:**

One DAX decision can make your model easier to maintain.

If a value depends on filter context, start by asking whether it belongs as a measure instead of a calculated column. Measures keep logic flexible and can reduce unnecessary model size.

The right choice still depends on the use case, but making the decision explicit prevents many performance and maintenance problems later.
