**Hook:** One CALCULATE tweak can halve your load time.

**Full Draft:**

One CALCULATE tweak can halve your load time.

I noticed a sales report taking 12 seconds to refresh. The culprit was a measure that applied FILTER inside CALCULATE for every row. By moving the filter to a VAR and using CALCULATE once, the query time dropped to 5 seconds.

Key pattern: define static filters in a variable, then apply CALCULATE with that variable. It forces early evaluation and reduces row‑by‑row work.

Try it on a slow visual and share your timing results!

## Idea 5 — Power BI & DAX
**Topic:** When to Use Measures vs. Calculated Columns

**Hook:** Choosing the wrong one slows every visual.

**Full Draft:**

Choosing the wrong one slows every visual.

Measures calculate on the fly, respecting the current filter context. Calculated columns, however, are evaluated once during data load and stored in memory. Use a column for static classifications, like “Region = IF(State=\"CA\", \"West\", \"Other\")”. Use a measure for anything that must react to slicers, such as “Revenue YoY = CALCULATE(SUM(Sales), SAMEPERIODLASTYEAR(Date))”.

Mixing them up leads to bloated models and sluggish dashboards. Review your model, move any context‑sensitive logic to measures.

**Why it works:** _Clarifies a frequent source of confusion and asks a direct question that invites peers to share their own pitfalls._
